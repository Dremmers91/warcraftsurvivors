# Complete ability catalog

Generated by `scripts/catalog.py` from the shipped JSON. The complete six-class roster remains active in Milestone 5; these are implemented spells rather than planned content.

All automatic damage uses the named school; enemies currently have no school resistances. Damage values below mean damage per collision, arc, pulse, companion attack, or DoT tick according to the behavior. Rank values are before passives, branches, manual buffs and evolution.

Each evolution replaces its original slot and preserves its Rank 8 state/branch. All evolved damage is multiplied by 1.65; the specific behavior transformation is shown for every entry. Evolution requires Rank 8, at least Rank 1 of the listed passive, and choosing that evolution when an elite chest is opened.

Automatic healing uses actual dealt damage (no overkill healing) and a shared 4%-maximum-health/second recovery budget, modified by healing talents. Boss wounds reduce life steal temporarily. Manual healing is separate. Gameplay effects and exact collision checks are preserved at every cosmetic density.

Maximum equipment: eight automatic slots, eight passive slots, one manual slot. Rank 4 unlocks a mutually exclusive Force (+30% damage, +15% cooldown) or Reach (+30% footprint) branch.

## Shared passives

All twelve have five ranks. Only eight can be equipped. Health and movement remain hero-only; there are no class resources.

### Multicast (`projectiles`)

+1 effect per rank. Projectile volleys, chains, DoT targets, orbiters and summons; no extra disks/arcs/fields/beams.

### Reach (`area`)

+20% attack area per rank. Projectile collision, melee reach, field/wave size, chain range and companion cleave. DoTs: spread/death explosion area only; no tick damage bonus.

### Might (`might`)

+15% damage per rank. All automatic damage, including DoT ticks and owner-bound companion attacks.

### Haste (`haste`)

-7% cooldown per rank. Automatic and manual cooldowns; companion attack interval. Does not shorten DoT tick interval.

### Endurance (`duration`)

+20% duration per rank. Fields, channels, DoTs, orbiters, temporary summons and manual timed effects; permanent pets never expire.

### Velocity (`velocity`)

+15% projectile speed per rank. Moving shots, moving fields and charging summons only; no permanent-pet movement or orbit rotation bonus.

### Fleetfoot (`movement`)

+8% movement speed per rank. Hero movement only; does not change projectile speed.

### Magnetism (`pickup`)

+30 pickup range per rank. XP, gold and chest attraction only.

### Fortitude (`armor`)

+8 percentage points damage reduction per rank, capped at 75%. Player contact damage only; companions cannot take damage.

### Vitality (`health`)

+20 maximum health and heal 20 per rank. Hero health only.

### Renewal (`regen`)

Regenerate 0.6 health per second per rank. Hero health only; pauses with gameplay.

### Penetration (`piercing`)

+1 enemy penetrated per rank. Traveling projectiles only; orbiters already strike repeatedly and do not benefit. Not disks, arcs, DoTs, beams or pets.

## Calculation notes

- Multicast affects projectile volleys, orbiters, chain hops, DoT target count and both summon types. It does not duplicate arcs, disks, fields or beams.
- Reach expands attacks. Velocity modifies moving projectiles, fields and chargers; orbit angular speed stays fixed. Penetration affects traveling projectiles only.
- Duration changes timed effects and manual buffs. A permanent companion has no lifetime; an evolved persistent field does not expire.
- Haste reduces automatic/manual cooldowns and permanent companion attack intervals; DoTs retain a one-second tick interval.
- Fire-only Combustion and shadow-only Dark Soul do not modify other schools. Bestial Wrath affects permanent pets and temporary chargers only.
- Lay on Hands Rank 1 restores full health; later ranks add shields. Guard effects and armor combine subject to the 75% mitigation cap.
- Blink, Disengage, Heroic Leap and Demonic Circle resolve destination obstacles and finite-world boundaries. Circle placement has no cooldown; returning triggers cooldown.
