# Approved milestone scope

Milestone 1 delivered the Paladin/Judgment core and its visual follow-up. Milestone 2 delivered six classes, 60 spells, 18 manuals, 12 passives, branching upgrades and 60 chest evolutions. Historical reports and requests remain under `docs/`.

Milestone 3 completes the initial Elwynn 30-minute campaign: 11 regular variants, 30 authored waves, three elite archetypes at five-minute intervals, usable evolution chests, Hogger, death/victory results, independent permanent rewards and developer encounter tools. See `MILESTONE3_REQUEST.md`, `ENCOUNTER_CATALOG.md`, `MILESTONE3_REPORT.md` and `QA.md`.

Milestone 4 implements permanent class levels, six 18-node talent trees, shared training, resets, achievements/registry unlocks, version 2 saves and full progression navigation. See `MILESTONE4_REQUEST.md`, `PROGRESSION_CATALOG.md`, `SAVE_FORMAT.md` and `MILESTONE4_REPORT.md`.

Milestone 5 adds Duskwood, Stranglethorn Vale, Eastern Plaguelands and Icecrown; five independent 30-minute schedules, original terrain/landmarks, 31 regular enemies, 11 elite variants, five iconic final bosses, original audio, polished settings/navigation, profile-led optimization and Windows release packaging. See `MILESTONE5_REQUEST.md`, `MILESTONE5_REPORT.md`, `ZONE_CATALOG.md` and current `QA.md`.

Multiple randomized boss entries per zone, new playable classes, multiplayer and further progression expansion remain deferred. Each zone ships one final boss in an extensible selection pool.

Do not proceed to another milestone automatically.
